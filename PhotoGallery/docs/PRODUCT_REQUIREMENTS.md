# PhotoGallery product requirements

The approved [stage 2 plan](STAGE2_PLAN.md) and
[current development guide](DEVELOPMENT_GUIDE_Eng.md) replace the initial August draft.

Acceptance: a real MediaContent-backed Gallery; Pictures/Albums/Favorites/search,
actual image detail, info, slideshow and confirmed owned-copy deletion; preserved
source files and stable IDs; full standard Photo 8, Custom 2 and View 4 Actions;
public API14 and immutable generated inheritance; uniform inset-aware scaling;
current View/Presentation contracts; independently reported host, build, packaging,
Action and Aurum gates; scoped artifact-free commit and push.
